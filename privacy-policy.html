1. Information We Collect & Local Save State

Bharat Finance Hub operates as a client-side functional engineering platform.

  • Local Storage (No-Login Data Retention): All calculations, inputs, and financial metrics entered into our 8 Master Workspace Tabs (including Personal Tax, SIP, FD, and Loan EMI calculators) are saved directly in your web browser's localStorage. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.
